Survey Flow

Ask every customer how it went. Survey Flow sends a short satisfaction survey when an item reaches the status you choose, then collects the ratings and comments where your team can read them. Nobody has to remember to send anything. Most teams use it for service tickets, but it works on any board with a status and an email address.
