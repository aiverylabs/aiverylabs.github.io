What Survey Flow does

Survey Flow watches a board you nominate. When an item reaches the status you choose, it emails the address in the field you mapped, inviting that person to rate the work from one to five stars and answer any follow up questions you configured. Responses appear on a dashboard in your monday.com account, broken down by agent, and can be written back onto the item.

The user guide describes the current feature set. Features change over time, and section 15 covers how.

We cannot honor a complaint against our sending domain selectively. This means an address you have never emailed may already be suppressed, and we will not tell you which, because that would reveal another customer's data.

We can throttle or suspend sending that puts the domain at risk. If your sending produces a bounce or complaint rate that threatens deliverability for other customers, we may rate limit or pause your sending.
